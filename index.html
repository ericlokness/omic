Our Story

A love story, churned slowly

Lifelong Washingtonians Karl and Bev Black have built a legacy that reflects their shared love of the environment, sweet desserts, dedication to family — and each other.

They met more than 45 years ago, in the hippie heyday, at a friendly potluck. Knowing glances over Bev’s chocolate-chip cookies turned into love at first sight; Karl was hooked at first bite. The two set to homesteading in the Skokomish Valley, hand-building their rustic dream cabin on fifteen acres of raw mountain-valley bliss while running a small local greenhouse business.

The valley became the birthplace and home of their three children — Trail, Alana, and Joel — and Karl and Bev marked their land with fields of ornamental Japanese maples and vibrant seasonal blooms.

Then, on a 1984 road trip to Seattle, a scoop of refreshing cantaloupe sorbet sparked an exciting new vision. With three young children at home, trading horticulture for food production was an intimidating leap that demanded plenty of soul-searching. Karl and Bev knew achieving their dream would be no simple task.

“Heavenly Gelato” soon opened in Lacey, WA, and the family immersed themselves in learning everything they could about making the best possible product. After two years, they realized their small mall storefront couldn’t support a dream of dozens of gourmet flavors. Still believing strongly in the product, they began selling wholesale to restaurants around Puget Sound — and found a foodie niche hungry for custom-crafted gourmet sorbets and ice cream. They renamed the business Olympic Mountain Ice Cream, applied their small-batch gelato craft, and launched their original line of super-premium ice creams.

More than thirty years later, Karl and Bev hold the same incredibly high standards — forever convinced that ice cream should be an indulgence worth savoring.

Today the children are all grown, and grandchildren play in the yard of the forested family home in the valley. In November 2026 the ice cream moved to a state-of-the-art new creamery at the Port of Shelton, with a shop where you can buy a scoop or a pint. The recipes haven’t changed, and it all continues one small batch at a time.

From the harvest, to the churn

Only the freshest ingredients go into our ice creams and sorbets. We buy our berries and stone fruit at their peak from Pacific Northwest growers, and nearly all of the herbs we use are grown on our own farm. We never settle for anything less than the finest — we love our ice cream, and we wish the same to you.

Good Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is your ice cream available in stores?

Yes! We currently offer select flavors in stores around the Puget Sound area. Check the Where to Find section for specifics.

Can I buy tubs of ice cream directly from you?

Yes! Our tub sizes are 1.5 gallon and 3 gallons. Our batches are run in 3-gallon amounts, requiring a 3-gallon minimum per flavor order — or give us a call to see if we have your flavor choice in stock in a smaller quantity.

Do you sell to the public?

Yes! Our new creamery has a shop where you can buy a scoop or a pint. Find us at 130 W Corporate Rd, Shelton, open 10am to 6pm, 7 days a week.

Does your ice cream contain gluten, eggs, or soy?

While our basic ice cream does not contain gluten, eggs, or soy, some of the inclusions we use do contain these ingredients. Please email or call to inquire about specific flavors.

Are your sorbets dairy and egg free?

Yes! Our sorbets are both egg and dairy free.

Do you give tours?

Unfortunately, at this time we are unable to offer tours of our facility.

What areas do you distribute to?

We deliver all around the Hood Canal area and Highway 101 north to Lilliwaup, all around Olympia, as far north on I-5 as Edmonds, and east to Redmond and Issaquah. We also work with the distributors US Foods, Food Services of America, Liberty Dairy, Medosweet, and Dairy Fresh Farms to reach outlying areas.

I own a business and would like to carry your products. How do I set up an account?

You can order pints, quarts, 1.5 and 3 gallon tubs and gelato pans on our wholesale ordering page, or give us a call at 360-426-0696 and we can help answer any questions you have about ordering and getting deliveries.
