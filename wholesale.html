      Wholesale FAQ

      Before you order

      Can I mix flavors and sizes in one order?

      Yes. Add each flavor and size as its own line. You can combine ice creams and sorbets in any mix of pints, quarts, 1.5 and 3 gallon tubs and gelato pans.

      What if the flavor I want isn’t listed?

      Type it into the flavor search and choose “Request as a custom flavor.” We love a challenge (we’ve even made a Curry Oyster ice cream) and we’ll confirm the details with you before churning.

      Are some flavors seasonal?

      Yes. Flavors tagged seasonal are made in limited batches around the Pacific Northwest harvest. If one is out of season we’ll call you about a substitute. Sorbets tagged booze are made with wine or spirits and cost extra.

      Do your products contain allergens?

      Our base ice cream doesn’t contain gluten, eggs or soy, but some inclusions do. All of our sorbets are dairy and egg free. Call 360-426-0696 about any specific flavor.

      Do I need an existing account to order?

      No. New businesses can order here directly. If you’d like to set up standing deliveries or talk through your menu, call us at 360-426-0696.
